Why I built
Mizan.

A community zakat calculator for Lunch Money and YNAB users.

My balances were already in Lunch Money.

I wanted to work out my zakat from those real balances, not from a guess. So I built Mizan. It works with YNAB too.

Your balances come in, and you decide what belongs. Untick an account, edit a balance, or add anything that isn't recorded, such as cash at home.

Check how the total adds up.

The breakdown lists each account and deduction, and marks anything you edited. The charts use those same amounts. You can also compare methods, or look back over the monthly balances that are available.

Check any balance marked "May be out of date" before using the estimate.

Choose the method you follow.

Scholars differ on points such as whether the nisab (the minimum you must own before zakat is due) is measured in gold or silver, and whether a mortgage is deducted. Mizan lets you choose and compare, and gives an estimate. Confirm your zakat method with your scholar.
