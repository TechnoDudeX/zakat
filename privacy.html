Who runs Mizan

Mizan is a free, open-source community project by Mazin Kanuga. It is a static website: there is no Mizan server, database or account. It is not made or supported by Lunch Money or YNAB.

What Mizan reads, and why

Mizan uses your data for one purpose only: to show you an estimate of your zakat and how it was calculated.

  • Lunch Money: your budget name and currency, your accounts and their balances, your crypto balances and your month-end balance history.
  • YNAB: your plan names and currency, your accounts and their balances, and the last 13 months of transactions. Transactions are used only to work out month-end balances for the hawl (the zakat year). Mizan asks YNAB for read-only access.

Mizan reads nothing else, and never changes anything in Lunch Money or YNAB.

How your data is handled

  • In your browser only. Your browser requests the data directly from Lunch Money (api.lunchmoney.dev) or YNAB (api.ynab.com) and does the calculation on your device. Nothing is sent to Mizan's author or to any Mizan server.
  • Your access. A Lunch Money token that you paste is cleared from the field before the first request. A YNAB sign-in gives the page a read-only access token that YNAB ends after 2 hours; the page removes it from the address bar straight away. In both cases the token is kept only in the page's memory while your balances load, then discarded. Mizan never sees your YNAB or Lunch Money password.
  • Not stored. Your balances, transactions and tokens are never saved: not in cookies, not in browser storage, not on a server. They are gone when you close or reload the page.
  • Saved on your device: only your calculation choices (nisab, mortgage method, nisab weights and metal purity), in your browser's local storage. During YNAB sign-in, a random one-time code is kept in session storage to check the sign-in, and is deleted when you return.
  • Secured. The site is served over HTTPS. A Content Security Policy lets the page connect only to Lunch Money, YNAB and the metal price service, and the page refuses redirects so a token cannot be sent elsewhere.

Third parties

Your Lunch Money or YNAB data is never passed to any third party, sold or used for anything else. To get today's gold and silver prices, the page asks gold-api.com for prices in your currency; that request contains only a currency code such as CAD, never your balances or token. The site is hosted on Netlify, which serves the files like any web host.

Deleting your data

Mizan holds no data about you, so there is nothing to delete on our side. To clear your saved calculation choices, choose Reset my method choices in the calculator or clear this site's data in your browser. To end Mizan's access, revoke your Lunch Money token on the Lunch Money developers page, or remove Mizan under Account Settings in YNAB. For any question or request, open an issue on GitHub.

Changes

If Mizan starts reading a new kind of data or uses it differently, this page will be updated first, with a new date above, and you will be asked to agree before the new data is read.
